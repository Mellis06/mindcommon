# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and constructive experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or point of view.

## Expected behaviour

- Treat people with respect and empathy.
- Listen to people with lived experience and do not pressure anyone to disclose personal information.
- Accept constructive criticism and explain disagreements without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Make space for perspectives that are often excluded from technology decisions.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, doxxing, unwanted disclosure of personal information, and exploitative or coercive conduct are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is acting as a project representative. Report concerns privately to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed with care and confidentiality to the extent possible. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation where necessary to protect people.

This project is maintained on a best-effort basis; a formal reporting contact and process should be added before expanding community participation.
